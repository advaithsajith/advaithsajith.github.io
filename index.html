V · Film & fashion

Every thread is a line of code.

I watch a lot of films, mostly horror and mysteries, and log every one on Letterboxd, rewinding to see where the director hid the twist. I am drawn to fashion and film for the same reason I love code: a dress and a program are both patterns, woven one thread at a time.

In 1804 the Jacquard loom wove patterns from punched cards, the idea that led to the first programmable machines. In FIRST LEGO League my team rebuilt a loom from LEGO. This dancer is drawn by about a hundred lines of JavaScript: watch her dress come apart into the threads it is made of.

IX · The type

Good design is mostly invisible spacing.

Writing, code and clothes all come down to spacing: where a sentence breaks, how a function is laid out, how a hem falls. Typographers call the space between two letters kerning, and readers only notice it when it goes wrong. The letters on this page are drawn with their guide lines showing.
